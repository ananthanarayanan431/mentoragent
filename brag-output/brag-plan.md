# Brag plan: Mentor Square (LinkedIn cut)

**What it is:** a pixel town where AI mentors, each grounded in their own published work, stroll around. You walk up and talk to one, they talk to each other about your goal, and you can join them.
**For:** founders and learners who want advice from several great minds at once.
**Angle:** "What if the sharpest minds shared one room?" We open on the questions, then answer each one with the real app.
**Mentors are unnamed.** They appear as archetypes (The Investor, The Strategist, The Inventor, The Contrarian, The Builder). The goal and every line are illustrative mock data, served by a mocked API. No real names, keys or user data.
**Format:** 1080×1080 square at 30fps, about 38.5s. Captions are burned in for muted autoplay.
**Tone:** polished, warm and curious. Pixel-game charm, no hype words.
**Identity:** the app's own UI with Pixelify Sans and Inter. Accent amber oklch(0.56 0.14 55), world green #2f4a2c, parchment dialogue.
**Music:** 120 BPM (beat = 0.5s), soft chiptune in A minor/C. Every cut lands on a beat.
